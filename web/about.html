The idea

See the room.
Understand the work.

Industrial context is scattered across physical spaces, device readings and maintenance documents. Scan2Work brings those pieces into a shared 3D environment.

  1. 01

    Scan

    Start with the supplied 3D Gaussian-splat capture of an industrial boiler room.

  2. 02

    Understand

    Frame individual assets and connect them to synthetic equipment records, conditions and telemetry.

    The equipment context
  3. 03

    Work

    Follow a technician’s route, surface nearby equipment and review evidence for the next inspection step.

The Valmet Automation challenge

Industrial Metaverse: Semantic 3D Scene Understanding & Autonomous Field-Service Copilot

Built at the Since AI 2026 hackathon in Turku, Finland, 6–8 November 2026, for Valmet Automation’s challenge.

About the hackathon ↗
01 / Scene understanding

Give objects an identity

Extract and frame assets from the supplied scene so equipment can be selected and understood in context.

02 / Data integration

Connect the records

Map the provided synthetic Excel equipment, telemetry, alarm and service data to the extracted assets.

03 / Spatial simulation

Make the route matter

Simulate obstacle-aware technician navigation and show nearby assets as the technician moves through the room.

04 / Grounded guidance

Explain the next step

Use device conditions and supplied troubleshooting documentation to support diagnostics with traceable evidence.
